# Historical character and material prompts

These prompts record the original painted character sheets and material experiments. The sheets are visual references; the released timer uses continuous 3D character models rather than raster atlases. See the [artwork notes](../ARTWORK.md) and [asset ledger](../../ASSETS.md) for the current implementation. The original prompt text follows for provenance.

## Painted transparent cat

Create a production raster character asset using the attached approved painting as the exact identity and medium reference. ONE full-body domestic shorthair tabby-and-white cat wearing the same vermilion artist beret, in a clear side view facing RIGHT, walking naturally with one near forepaw lifted forward to push debris. Entire animal and gracefully curling tail visible with generous transparent margin. Match the reference cat's elegant credible feline anatomy, small green eyes, ochre/brown/cream markings, richly layered fine-art oil-and-gouache brushwork, visible pigment, textured edges. A gallery-quality painting, NOT vector, NOT cartoon icon, NOT plastic 3D render. Isolate only the cat: genuinely transparent background, no floor, no shadow, no bowl, no fish, no plants, no text. Keep paws clear and separated so this single approved character asset can be gently articulated in an animation rig. One single still pose, not a sprite sheet, no additional cats.

## Frosted glass texture

Production material texture for an approved artistic classroom clock. One square, flat front-on scan of a luminous fine-grained sandblasted frosted glass pane. No clock, no cat, no marks, no frame, no objects, no writing. Physical softly granular glass surface with subtle microscopic irregular pits, gentle cloudy mottling and diffused light transmission, pale ivory with an extremely faint sage-green undertone, fairly even brightness across the square. Rich tactile surface like high-quality frosted glass, delicate enough that dark painted clock hands composited above remain readable. Quiet sophisticated artistic realism, not paper, not cloth, not cracked glass, no glitter, no sharp geometric patterns. Full-bleed square texture. This is one material still, not animation frames.

## Cast approval sheet 1

Create ONE approval contact sheet of SIX high-quality individually painted animal character stills, in a precise 3-column by 2-row grid, one animal per cell, all fully visible with comfortable margins and no overlaps. Top row left to right: dog, panda, turtle. Bottom row left to right: pig, bear, cow. Place a small understated species-name label beneath each animal. Every animal faces right in a credible natural walking, flopping or flying pose as appropriate, ready to push debris for a classroom timer. This is a still design sheet, NOT an animation sequence: exactly one pose per animal. Match the attached approved cat painting's exquisite oil-and-gouache medium, rich hand-painted layered pigment, bristle brushwork, warm ochre and cream, dark forest-green and muted teal, occasional vermilion accents, textured painted canvas, sophisticated Matisse-inspired expressive contours. Each species must be immediately distinguishable by credible anatomy and characteristic details; small expressive eyes, no baby cartoon proportions, no vector art, no flat icons, no plastic 3D. Pale warm cream background in each cell. No costumes, no extra animals, no scenery, no clock. Keep painting quality and style consistent across all six and with the reference. Landscape image, high detail.
